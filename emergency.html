Emergency Notice

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Wellora does not provide emergency medical services.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, seek immediate medical assistance.

Call Emergency Services For

  • Chest pain
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe dizziness
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe pain
  • Signs of stroke

Who To Contact

Contact local emergency services, your nearest hospital, or your healthcare provider immediately.
