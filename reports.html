EmailJS Setup

  1. Sign up at emailjs.com.
  2. Create an email service and email template in the EmailJS dashboard.
  3. Add your values to `window.WELLORA_EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY`, `window.WELLORA_EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID`, and `window.WELLORA_EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID`, or store them in localStorage with the same names.

If EmailJS is not configured, Wellora will still let you generate, print, and download the report manually.
