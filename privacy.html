Wellora Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Information We Collect

Wellora collects only the information needed to provide the service: your email address for account creation and your age for personalisation.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide Wellora, personalise exercise recommendations, support account access, and send weekly summaries related to your wellness activity.

Confirmation Emails

Account confirmation emails are sent through Supabase Auth. These messages are secure account emails and are not third-party email marketing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. We do not use personal data for advertising or third-party marketing.

User Rights

You may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your account and related data by contacting us.

Retention

We keep your email and age until your account is deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Contact

For privacy requests, email favourosofisan@gmail.com.
